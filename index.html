The Sparrow's Baskets

A Halloween story in five stops. At the end, you choose a basket and pack two for a friend.

Long ago, a kind old man kept a sparrow.

His wife, in a temper, cut out its tongue. The sparrow flew off into the night.

The old man went looking for it. Tonight, so do you.

Lantern Lane

Paper lanterns hang the length of the lane, humming in the rain.

Most of them are only lanterns.

One of them has been watching you since the gate.

Don't look at it. It licks.

At the crossroads, red spider lilies push up through the concrete. They grow where the living world wears thin.

An old umbrella hops past on one leg.
